Marisol Heat is a top-down 2D open-world crime game set in the fictional city of Marisol. Explore five districts on foot or behind the wheel, steal cars, dodge traffic, run six story missions for Mama Luz and Rico, fight guards with fists and a pistol, and outrun the Marisol PD as your wanted level climbs from zero to five stars. The whole game runs offline in a desktop browser — no libraries, no network, just a canvas, a city grid and a WebAudio synth for engine hums, sirens and gunshots.

## Features

- Open city: five districts (Old Town, Harbour, Market Row, Palm Heights, Downtown), road grid, traffic AI, pedestrians who flee from trouble
- Drive and steal: five car models (Pinto, Sedan, Taxi, Pickup, Falcon GT) with different speed, grip and health; any parked car can be taken with E
- Wanted system: 0–5 stars, police cruisers chase and ram you, heat decays when you break line of sight
- Six chained missions: deliveries, a grand-theft-auto job, a car chase against Rico, a warehouse fight and a final five-star escape
- Combat: punch (F) and pistol (G) with limited ammo, guard AI that chases and hits back
- HUD + minimap, start/pause screens, autosave after every mission (localStorage), WebAudio sound effects

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

Controls: WASD move/drive · E enter/exit car · F punch · G shoot · P pause · M mute.
